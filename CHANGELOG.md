# Thermal Print Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- To-Do List: add tasks from the search bar with natural-language due dates, then print the whole list as one receipt grouped by day
- Add To-Do and Print To-Do List for quick capture and hotkey printing
- Compose Receipt, Print Selection or Clipboard (works with Raycast Notes), Receipt Library and Printer Status
- Pixel-accurate receipt previews, auto-detection of Epson printers, and a Pending queue for jobs that couldn't print
- Print Image: photos, screenshots and any image file, dithered for thermal paper; images also print from Finder, the clipboard and Compose
- A general document model (text, markdown, lists, tables, images, QR codes, barcodes, cuts) behind every receipt
